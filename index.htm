Methods of preparation of animal meat

Animal meat products (pork, beef, chicken, grass carp and golden pomfret) were processed as controls. Fresh meats were deboned, manually defatted, and homogenized through a meat grinder. The mince meats were blended with 0 g/100 g and 10 g/100 g of SPI evenly, and then molded into standardized round patties (3 cm diameter × 1 cm height). Subsequently, these raw meat were steamed for 20 min, and then cooled at room temperature for 2 h. Finally, these meat products were stored at 4℃.
